## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro with static output. The pages are content-led landing pages that need fast first loads on mobile, small client-side forms, and a reusable component set so each new campaign page can be put together quickly. Astro ships zero JS by default, builds to plain static files that can be hosted anywhere, and replaces the unmaintained Jekyll + Gulp 3 template now in the repo. The hosting / deploy target has not been decided yet.

## Users

Two audiences, each with its own path to a quote:

- **Residential:** property owners in Dubai planning a renovation, fit-out or interior design project for an apartment, villa or townhouse.
- **Commercial:** operators of clinics, restaurants and offices in Dubai who need a fit-out or renovation of their premises.

Both arrive on a landing page (typically from an ad, search or social link), and their job is to decide whether Haus Techs is credible and right for their property, then get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Landing pages for **Haus Techs**, a renovation, fit-out and interior design company in Dubai. They exist to turn visitors into enquiries. Success means qualified leads through any of the supported contact channels.

## Positioning

Open: the specific claim that sets Haus Techs apart from other Dubai renovation contractors has not been confirmed yet. Do not invent one; ask the owner before writing positioning copy.

## Operating Context

- Visitors are mostly on mobile and often arrive from paid campaigns. Each landing page usually targets one property type or service.
- Enquiries lead into Haus Techs' quoting process. Quotations, packages and per-sqm pricing come from the company's own rate library and are prepared after the enquiry, not shown as fixed prices on the page unless the owner provides them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Contact channels (all four are required):** WhatsApp chat, a quote-request form, booking a site visit or call, and tap-to-call. Each page offers them, with the right one leading for that page's audience.
- **Services:** renovation, fit-out and interior design for residential properties (apartments, villas, townhouses) and commercial ones (clinics, restaurants, offices).
- **Undecided:** where form submissions go (email, CRM, sheet), the WhatsApp number, the booking tool, and the phone number. Use clearly marked placeholders until the owner supplies them.
- **Undecided:** language support (English only, or English + Arabic with RTL).
- The existing repo content (Travis Neilson's 2015 personal-site template, `tn-2015.psd`, the lorem-ipsum sections and assets) is legacy scaffolding. It is not Haus Techs content or brand.

## Product Principles

1. **Every page earns one enquiry.** All four contact channels are always within reach, and the page's main channel leads.
2. **Proof is real or absent.** Show only the owner's actual photos and reviews. Never use stock imagery passed off as their projects, invented testimonials or made-up numbers.
3. **Speak to the property in front of the visitor.** Residential and commercial visitors have different stakes, so each page addresses one audience and property type directly.
4. **Mobile-first and fast.** Most traffic is mobile ad clicks, so load speed and thumb-reachable actions come first.
